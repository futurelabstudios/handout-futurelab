# AI At Scale

India's AI conversation has moved from experimentation to operating-model change. Deloitte India's 2026 AI material reports strong at-scale adoption momentum, while noting capability gaps in agentic, physical, and generative AI expertise.

## Current Synthesis

The client agenda is shifting toward activation: use-case portfolios, AI governance, value tracking, data readiness, human workflow redesign, model risk, cyber controls, and scaling infrastructure. The central question is no longer "can we pilot AI?" but "can we make AI reliable, governed, and value-accretive across functions?"

## Implications For Deloitte India

- Consulting and technology teams need repeatable AI operating-model patterns.
- Deloitte India's "Human+" mainframe modernisation perspective frames AI as an accelerator for discovery, dependency mapping, implementation, validation, and AIOps, with human specialists preserving business intent, regulatory fit, and safe release sequencing.
- Risk, audit, and cyber teams need control frameworks for AI-generated work and agentic workflows.
- Tax and finance functions need AI-enabled transformation while preserving explainability and auditability.
- Talent teams need upskilling because expertise gaps can become delivery bottlenecks.

## Client Conversation Starters

- Which AI use cases are already in production, and where is value leakage happening?
- Which processes need human-in-the-loop controls before automation expands?
- What should be governed centrally versus owned by business functions?
- How will AI adoption change skills, roles, and vendor risk?
