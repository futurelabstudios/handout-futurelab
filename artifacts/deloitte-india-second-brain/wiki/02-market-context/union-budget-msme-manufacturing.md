# Union Budget MSME and Manufacturing Signals

The 2026-27 Union Budget signals an industrial capacity agenda: MSME scale-up, manufacturing hubs, infrastructure, and future-skill pipelines.

## Current Synthesis

Public government briefings emphasize MSMEs as central to growth, with more than 7.47 crore enterprises and 32.82 crore workers, and a major role in manufacturing output, exports, and GDP. Budget measures include equity support, liquidity, professional support, and courier-export facilitation.

Manufacturing policy is also shifting toward integrated hubs and shared infrastructure, including chemical parks, PM MITRA textile parks, MSME clusters, and Biopharma SHAKTI.

## Why It Matters For Deloitte India

- Strategy and operations teams can map cluster-led growth opportunities by sector and state.
- Tax and trade teams should track customs, GST, export, and incentive implications.
- Risk and audit teams can support governance, controls, and supply-chain resilience as MSMEs formalize.
- Technology and transformation teams can connect manufacturing hubs with ERP, data, AI, cybersecurity, and digital factory programs.
