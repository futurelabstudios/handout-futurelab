# Consumer Signals India

Indian consumer demand remains resilient, but value discipline is shaping where spending grows.

## Current Synthesis

Deloitte India's May 2026 Consumer Signals India Chapter points to a consumer base that is financially steadier than global peers, but more selective. Spending intent is cautious, large purchases are being deferred, and discretionary growth is concentrating in experience-led categories where value, comfort, reliability, and quality are visible.

This connects with an earlier Deloitte India retail view: consumer and retail clients need commercial models that can handle value-seeking, selective premiumisation, localised innovation, and experience-led demand.

## Why It Matters For Deloitte India

- Consumer and retail clients may need sharper pricing, assortment, channel, loyalty, and working-capital strategies.
- Automotive and mobility clients should watch softer vehicle purchase intent alongside rising EV interest.
- Travel, hospitality, leisure, and food-service clients can use experience-led demand, but need value clarity.
- Data, AI, and customer analytics can help segment cautious consumers without over-discounting.

## Client Conversation Starters

- Which categories are trading volume for value, and which can still support premiumisation?
- Where are customers delaying large purchases, and what financing or bundling models reduce friction?
- Which experience-led categories can absorb targeted investment?
- How should AI-driven demand forecasting, pricing, and personalisation be governed?
