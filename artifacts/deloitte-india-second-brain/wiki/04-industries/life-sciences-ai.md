# Life Sciences AI

Life sciences AI is moving from isolated productivity use cases toward regulated, evidence-heavy workflows.

## Current Synthesis

Recent Deloitte material frames AI in life sciences around outcome-driven managed services, regulatory intelligence, R&D productivity, medical and commercial workflows, and the operating changes needed when business and IT teams share process ownership.

For India-facing teams, the signal is not just "AI for pharma". It is AI plus validation, data governance, privacy, quality, pharmacovigilance, regulatory intelligence, and audit-ready evidence.

## Why It Matters For Deloitte India

- Pharma and life sciences clients need AI roadmaps that respect validation and regulatory constraints.
- Data and AI teams can support evidence platforms, regulatory intelligence, and workflow automation.
- Risk, cyber, privacy, and assurance teams should be built into AI programs early.
- GCC and capability-centre teams may become important execution hubs for global life sciences AI operations.

## Watch Items

- AI-assisted regulatory intelligence and submission workflows.
- Data quality and lineage for real-world data and evidence.
- Model governance for medical, commercial, safety, and compliance use cases.
- Cross-border privacy and data-transfer issues where patient, trial, or health data is involved.
