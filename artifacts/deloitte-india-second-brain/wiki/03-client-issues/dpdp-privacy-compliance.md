# DPDP Privacy Compliance

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and Rules create a phased compliance runway. The most important operating point is that privacy readiness is a transformation program, not a one-time legal memo.

## Current Synthesis

The DPDP framework establishes consent-based processing of digital personal data, with rules that phase into force over time. NIST's crosswalk notes that the Act was enacted on 11 August 2023, the Rules were notified in November 2025, and enforcement is phased across immediate, 12-month, and 18-month milestones.

Deloitte India's DPDP point of view frames the rules as a privacy compliance agenda involving notices, consent, security safeguards, breach handling, children's data, data principal rights, retention, cross-border transfer readiness, and significant data fiduciary governance.

## Deloitte India Relevance

- Cyber and privacy teams can lead data discovery, gap assessment, governance design, and privacy operations.
- Technology teams can support consent, preference, data lineage, retention, and rights-management tooling.
- Risk and internal-audit teams can test controls and evidence.
- Industry teams should tailor impacts for financial services, consumer, health, education, telecom, and digital platforms.

## Watch Items

- Government orders on cross-border transfer conditions.
- Consent manager operational requirements.
- Data Protection Board procedures and enforcement practice.
- Interaction with AI governance, cyber incident reporting, and sectoral regulations.
