# North Star

The Deloitte India second brain exists to make useful professional context compound. It should help teams move from "what changed?" to "what should we discuss with clients?" without re-reading the internet from scratch.

## Design Principles

- Compile first: sources become durable synthesis pages, not just search results.
- Preserve provenance: every important claim links back to a public source.
- Connect across functions: tax, cyber, AI, industry, risk, audit, and consulting signals should cross-link.
- Promote only durable signals: the inbox can be noisy, but topic pages should stay clean.
- Keep pages briefing-sized: split pages when they become too long to scan.
- Use a human review loop for regulated topics.

## Deloitte India Lens

The knowledge base should prioritize signals that affect:

- Client demand in India and South Asia.
- AI, data, cyber, privacy, tax, audit, assurance, risk, transactions, and transformation work.
- Sector shifts in technology, media, telecom, financial services, manufacturing, consumer, government, energy, and life sciences.
- Regulatory deadlines and compliance operating changes.
- Talent and delivery capabilities for professional services teams.
