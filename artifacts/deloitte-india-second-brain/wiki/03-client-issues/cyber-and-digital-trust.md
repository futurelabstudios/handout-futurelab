# Cyber and Digital Trust

Cybersecurity is now a strategic trust topic across AI, cloud, data, regulatory compliance, and operational resilience.

## Current Synthesis

Deloitte India's cyber services organize around cyber defence and resilience, cyber operate, cyber strategy and transformation, digital trust and privacy, and enterprise security. For Deloitte India professionals, the most useful lens is to connect cyber with business transformation instead of treating it as a technical afterthought.

## Why It Matters Now

- AI adoption increases data, model, prompt, identity, and third-party risks.
- DPDP creates privacy operations and breach-readiness pressure.
- Regulators are raising cyber-resilience expectations in financial markets and other sectors.
- SEBI's May 2026 AI vulnerability advisory makes AI-assisted discovery, patch prioritisation, API security, SOC monitoring, and third-party application risk board-relevant for capital-market participants.
- Deloitte's banking cyber analysis points to a wider shift: frontier AI can accelerate vulnerability discovery faster than traditional remediation processes can respond.
- Manufacturing and infrastructure digitization expands operational technology exposure.
- Quantum readiness is moving from theory to long-range cryptography planning.

## Client Conversation Starters

- What are the crown-jewel processes and data assets in the transformation roadmap?
- Are AI tools covered by existing identity, access, data-loss, logging, and vendor-risk controls?
- What is the cyber recovery path for an operational disruption?
- Which controls need continuous evidence for audit, board, or regulator reporting?
