# Financial Services Digital Banking

Digital banking maturity is now a strategy, technology, trust, and regulatory execution question.

## Current Synthesis

Deloitte's Digital Banking Maturity research positions digital maturity as a practical benchmark for how banks respond to changing customer expectations, digital journeys, ecosystem plays, analytics, and operational transformation.

For Deloitte India professionals, this connects to RBI regulatory expectations, UPI and digital payments growth, AI-enabled servicing, cyber resilience, DPDP readiness, and the need to modernise banking platforms without weakening controls.

Deloitte's 14 May 2026 banking cyber analysis adds an operating-risk lens: as frontier AI improves vulnerability discovery, banks may need faster triage, remediation execution, compensating architectural controls, and governance that can make risk decisions at machine-accelerated pace.

## Why It Matters For Deloitte India

- Banks and NBFCs need customer-experience transformation tied to risk, conduct, and operational resilience.
- AI and analytics should be assessed with privacy, model risk, and explainability controls.
- Digital lending, co-lending, embedded finance, and payments need regulator-aware operating models.
- Cyber and identity architecture become central to customer trust.

## Client Conversation Starters

- Which digital journeys lag market expectations and regulatory expectations?
- Where can AI improve service quality without increasing model, conduct, or privacy risk?
- What controls are needed for partner-led or embedded finance journeys?
- How quickly can vulnerability findings move from detection to risk-ranked remediation or compensating controls?
- How should digital maturity be measured across channels, data, tech, operations, and governance?
