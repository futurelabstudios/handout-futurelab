# GBrain Pattern

Garry Tan's GBrain approach treats the knowledge base as a readable brain repo plus a retrieval layer and agent skills. The useful transferable ideas are not the exact infrastructure; they are the operating habits.

## Transferable Ideas

- Compiled truth pages: maintain one best-current page for important concepts, people, companies, issues, and decisions.
- Timelines: record dated developments so the team can see how a topic evolved.
- Typed links: connect pages with relationships such as "regulates", "affects", "supports", "contradicts", and "updates".
- Recurring jobs: let the brain improve while people sleep, then review promoted changes.
- Skills: convert repeated workflows into explicit instructions so quality improves over time.
- Search hybridization: use keyword search, backlinks, and source freshness together.

## How It Applies Here

This prototype keeps the readable Markdown repo as the source of truth and adds a deterministic scanner for public internet signals. A scheduled Codex job can act as the more judgment-heavy layer: promote or reject signals, update synthesis pages, and add expert-review flags.
