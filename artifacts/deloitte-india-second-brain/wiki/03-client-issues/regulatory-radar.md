# Regulatory Radar

This page tracks cross-sector Indian regulatory signals that Deloitte India professionals may need to translate into client conversations.

## Active Watchlist

| Theme | Signal | Professional relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI governance | MeitY released India AI Governance Guidelines under the IndiaAI Mission. | Responsible AI, controls, model governance, procurement, and assurance. |
| Privacy | DPDP Act and Rules are moving through phased enforcement. | Data mapping, consent, breach response, rights management, and evidence. |
| Financial services | RBI Co-Lending Arrangements Directions 2025 came into force from 1 January 2026 for new arrangements. | Lending partnerships, conduct, disclosures, KYC, audit, and business continuity. |
| Capital markets | SEBI constituted a working group to review ESG rating provider regulation. | ESG ratings, capital-market compliance, assurance, investor trust. |
| Capital markets cyber | SEBI issued a 5 May 2026 advisory on advanced AI tools for vulnerability detection. | AI-enabled vulnerability discovery is becoming a regulatory cyber-resilience topic for market infrastructure institutions and regulated entities. |
| Indirect tax | GST Council continues to publish decisions, newsletters, and FAQs. | Tax operations, invoice systems, appeals, and sector-specific GST positions. |

## Operating Guidance

- Promote items to dedicated pages when there is a deadline, material client impact, or repeated mention.
- Keep media reports in the inbox until official circulars, regulator releases, or Deloitte analysis are available.
- For legal, tax, or audit topics, flag "needs specialist review" before using in client material.
