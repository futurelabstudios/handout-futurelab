# TMT 2026 India

India's technology, media, and telecommunications sector is moving beyond digital adoption into infrastructure scale, local behavior adaptation, and new monetization models.

## Current Synthesis

Deloitte India's TMT Predictions 2026 describes an ecosystem powered by digital infrastructure, AI compute, semiconductors, data centres, telecom super-app models, generative video formats, and a fast-rising live/concert economy.

The global Deloitte TMT view adds that agentic AI, AI search, AI compute, semiconductor supply-chain fragility, micro-dramas, video podcasts, and telecom reward ecosystems will reshape markets in 2026.

## India-Specific Implications

- AI compute and data localisation create cloud, data centre, power, land, and sustainability questions.
- Green data centre design is becoming a core AI infrastructure topic because power density, cooling, renewable supply, and efficiency directly affect AI economics.
- Semiconductor build-out connects to manufacturing policy, skills, incentives, and supply-chain resilience.
- Telcos may evolve from connectivity into embedded consumer and enterprise platforms.
- Media formats are becoming creator-led, multilingual, mobile-first, and AI-assisted.
- Trust, content authenticity, and cyber controls become commercial differentiators.

## Client Conversation Starters

- Which AI workloads should run in cloud, domestic data centres, or on-premise infrastructure?
- What partnerships can help telcos monetize beyond connectivity?
- How should media and platform clients manage AI-generated content risk?
- Where do semiconductor and electronics policies create strategy, tax, or operating-model opportunities?
