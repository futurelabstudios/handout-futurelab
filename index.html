The brief

The workshop's central message: AI adoption becomes real when teams turn tools into repeatable work systems.

Debrief

The workshop was not about using more AI. It was about making AI useful inside real work.

The core shift for Deloitte USI is moving from occasional tool use to repeatable work systems: better prompts, richer context, reusable knowledge, human review, and visible prototypes that prove value before scaling.

1
Prompt like a manager, not like a search user.

Give the model role, context, evidence, output format, and review criteria.

2
Turn knowledge into infrastructure.

Notes, meetings, links, reports, decisions, and prompts should compound into a second brain.

3
Prototype before debating.

A small working artifact is the fastest way to test trust, usability, controls, and business value.

Capstone behavior

Pick one workflow. Improve it with AI. Prove the delta.

The PDF's capstone instruction is the right adoption unit: identify a real workflow and improve it using the methods shared through the cohort.

Mental models

The simplest ideas are the most reusable: context, vehicles, engines, and maturity.

Mental model 01

Contextual prompting: from request to work contract

Naive prompt

"Summarize this."

Expert prompt

"You are a consulting manager preparing a board-level synthesis. Focus on strategic implications, differentiation, evidence quality, and executive readability."

The difference is not eloquence. It is context, audience, stakes, and review criteria.

Mental model 02

Engines versus vehicles

E
LLMs are engines.

Models such as ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Sarvam, and tokenizers provide capability.

V
Apps are vehicles.

ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, Perplexity, Genspark, and NotebookLM package capability into workflows.

Mental model 03

AI maturity ladder

Level 1

Chatbots and conversational language.

Level 2

Reasoners for human-level problem solving.

Level 3

Agents that can take actions across workflows.

Level 4

Innovators that can aid invention.

Level 5

Organizations where AI can do the work of an org.

The second brain

The point is not storage. It is a living knowledge base that reads, links, answers, and writes back.

Mental model 04

Second brain architecture

1
Inputs

Documents, emails, meetings, notes, links, and decisions.

2
Knowledge engineer

AI reads, cleans, links, summarizes, deduplicates, and extracts insight.

3
Living knowledge base

People, projects, ideas, decisions, timelines, and open questions.

4
Writes back

Useful answers update pages instead of disappearing inside chat history.

Mental model 05

RAG as governed retrieval

Retrieval augmented generation matters when answers need evidence. The practical loop is simple: source material, ingest and clean, retrieve the right context, answer with citations, then save validated learning back into the system.

Source trail Context window Review gate Reusable answer

Operating habit

Prompt, structure, build, review

P
Prompt

Clarify role, audience, source, task, and output.

S
Structure

Use JSON, schemas, tables, checklists, and skill files when work repeats.

B
Build

Make a prototype or artifact quickly enough for reaction.

R
Review

Check source quality, confidentiality, logic, and human ownership.

Prototype agenda
