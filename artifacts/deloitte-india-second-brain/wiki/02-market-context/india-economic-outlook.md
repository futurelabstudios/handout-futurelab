# India Economic Outlook

India entered 2026 with resilient domestic demand, stronger investment momentum, and reform-linked upside, while still facing trade and capital-flow volatility.

## Current Synthesis

Deloitte's India economic outlook expects fiscal 2025-26 growth to be revised upward to around 7.5% to 7.8%, then moderate to roughly 6.6% to 6.9% in fiscal 2026-27. The outlook points to festive spending, private consumption, investment, GST changes, and slowing inflation as important supports.

## Why It Matters For Deloitte India

- Growth resilience supports transformation demand, especially in consumer, industrial, financial services, and public sector contexts.
- Moderating but strong growth makes productivity, working capital, supply-chain resilience, and AI-led cost transformation board-level topics.
- Trade recalibration and fiscal discipline create openings for tax, indirect tax, risk, and strategy conversations.

## Watch Items

- Whether consumption strength persists after the festive and rural demand cycle.
- How GST changes flow into pricing, compliance, and sector margins.
- Whether private capex broadens beyond large corporates.
- Impact of global tariffs and currency volatility on export-heavy clients.
